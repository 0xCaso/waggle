How it works

Keep your agents moving without watching every terminal.

1

Your agent asks

When it hits a real choice, it sends a request: what it needs, why, the options and its pick.

2

It lands in Needs you

One list for every agent and project. For a review, click through the prototype and pin comments on it.

3

You answer, it keeps going

Your answer goes back to the same session. No copy and paste, no hunting for the right tab.

Stays on your Mac
