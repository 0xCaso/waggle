# Waggle 0.2.51

- **Share a case with your team.** Turn on Sharing in Settings, then choose Share in a case. Your team reads the case in a browser and comments on a text or an area. Sharing is off until you turn it on. With Sharing off, Waggle makes no network call for your cases.
- **Waggle accounts.** Sign in with an email link or Google to share. The Mac connects through your browser with a short code. Settings shows every link you share, from any Mac, and you can stop each one there.
- **Choose who can read.** Share with anyone who has the link, or with a list of emails. Each person on the list gets one invite mail. You can change the mode or the list later, and the link and the comments stay.
- **Comments come back to you and your agent.** New comments show in the case under From your team, with a notice and an unread mark. You can reply and resolve. Your agent sees the new comments as input from people, never as an answer.
- **The page follows the case.** Each new round goes up by itself, the page shows your decision after you answer, and you can close comments.
