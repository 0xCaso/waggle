# Knock 0.1.57

*Your agents knock before they ship. Defuse the slop before it lands.*

**New**

- Comment mode zooms and pans. Pinch, zoom, Fit, scroll and Space+drag work while you comment on a prototype or a diagram, and your pins stay on their spot at any zoom.
- Pins show at every zoom in Interact mode too.

**Fixed**

- A plan step reads like a readme. Only its first sentence is the headline, and the rest shows as normal text with lists and paragraphs.
