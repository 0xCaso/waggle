# Knock 0.1.31

*Your agents knock before they ship. Defuse the slop before it lands.*

**One page for prototypes and screenshots**

- **The design and the question side by side.** An ask with a prototype or screenshots now opens straight on one page: the queue on the left, the design in the middle, the question and your answer on the right. No more extra step to open the review.
- **Make room when you need it.** Fold the queue with ⌘[, fold the question column with ⌘], or go full preview with ⌘\ (Esc to come back). Knock remembers how you left it.

**See every option in full**

- **A new "Decision" section** lists each option with its whole text, so nothing is cut off. The answer box below shows a short title for each option. Agents can now send that title; for older agents Knock makes one from the option.

**Notifications in setup**

- **A new setup step** turns on notifications and lets you pick the sound. Click a sound to hear it and see a sample badge, or pick no sound.

**What you click, kept on your Mac**

- **`knock report` now has a "What you click" section**: which buttons and views you use, in counts only. It stays on your Mac. Share it only if you want to.

**Fixed**

- **The screenshot header no longer gets cut** on narrow windows.

**After updating:** nothing to do.
