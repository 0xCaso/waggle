# Waggle 0.2.41

- **A simpler Home.** When nothing needs you, Waggle now shows three small pictures of what it is for: plan, prototype variants, decide. There is nothing to click there. Your agent brings the work, and it lands in the sidebar.
- **"New idea" is gone.** The row at the top of the sidebar, the idea box and the menu item "New idea with a file" are removed. Start the work in your agent's session, as before.
