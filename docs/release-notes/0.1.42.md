# Knock 0.1.42

*Your agents knock before they ship. Defuse the slop before it lands.*

**A calmer right pane**

- One **Context** card replaces the thread and session blocks: one line per step, open any line for its detail, and the agent's newest update stays readable at the bottom.
- **Need from you** and **Why I can't decide** sit together, then a **Decision** card lists every option with a short description.
- The answer box is docked to the bottom of the pane, and the recommended option shows its reason right there.

**Ask for more detail, faster**

- "Ask for more detail" is the last row of the options (press **?**). It swaps the options for quick asks: trade-offs, a prototype, the numbers, an example, or something else in your own words.

**Fixes**

- A new ask opens on its own when nothing is selected. It never takes over an ask you are reading.
- An open ask shows the agent's new prototype or screenshots at once, without switching asks.
- The "What comes to Knock" track fills all the way at Everything.
