# Knock 0.1.28

*Your agents knock before they ship. Defuse the slop before it lands.*

**The ask and its conversation, side by side**

- **Split view.** Once you have replied to an ask, it opens in two columns: on the left what you decide on now (the question, the recommendation, the latest version and your answer), on the right the conversation with the agent, like a chat: its questions and updates on the left, your replies on the right, your turn at the bottom. On a narrow window the conversation folds into a "Conversation" button.
- **See the older version.** An update that brought a new version has a "v1 · see in review" link.

**Easier to read**

- **Agent messages render properly.** Bold, bullet lists, numbered lists, paragraphs and code now show as formatted text instead of raw symbols. Links show as text and never open, and nothing is loaded.
- **Timers in whole seconds**: "1m 36s", not "1m 36.4s".
- **Status lines line up**: the grid, the label and the timer sit on one line.

**Fixed**

- **The sessions panel fits in the window.** A session with many asks scrolls inside the panel instead of running off the top, and each ask shows its real state (Needs you, Working, Writing, Not picked up yet, or Answered).
