# Knock 0.1.32

*Your agents knock before they ship. Defuse the slop before it lands.*

**See where your answer is**

- **Your answer becomes a live status card.** After you send an answer on a prototype or screenshots ask, the answer box turns into "You picked" with your choice, a live status (Not picked up yet, Working, or Writing the detail you asked for) with its timer, and "Change answer". Nudge and Copy prompt show there only while the agent has not picked up yet.

**Fold the page your way**

- **Each side folds itself.** The queue and the question column each have their own panel button. Hover it to see the arrow. A folded side leaves a slim rail: the queue rail shows how many asks need you, the question rail shows the question. Click the rail to bring it back. ⌘[ and ⌘] still work.

**Comments stay on the page**

- **Pins stay visible in Interact mode**, not only while you comment. Hover a pin to read it, click it to open it.

**Fixed**

- **Short options no longer repeat themselves** in the Decision section.
- **The status timer no longer gets cut off** when it grows longer.

**After updating:** nothing to do.
