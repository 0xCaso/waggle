# Waggle 0.2.52

- **Sign-in finishes by itself.** After you approve the code in your browser, Settings > Account shows your account. You no longer choose Sign in a second time. The browser page now says the Mac is connected and has a Back to Waggle button.
- **One Comments tab.** Comments from your team and comments to your agent are in one tab. Choose To the agent or On the page when you write.
- **A clearer share sheet.** The link is at the top with a big Copy link button. Under it you see who can read, a switch to open or close comments, and the number of comments with a View button.
- **The shared page puts the artifact first.** The artifact fills the window. The latest round shows first, and earlier rounds are in a menu. Choose Expand (or press F) to see the artifact full screen, or open it in a new tab.
- **A new home page on the web.** After you sign in on app.waggle.so, you see your cases in two groups, with your account at the top.
- **The Waggle logo** is now on every web page.
