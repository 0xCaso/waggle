# Waggle 0.2.27

- **A new start.** Setup is now two short steps: connect your agent, then pick a project and a first job. Waggle sends the job to your open session, or starts one in Terminal.
- **Your first decision, in the real window.** While the agent works you see it in the queue. When its first request arrives, a short tour shows what the agent brings, how to decide or keep talking, and what a new round is.
- **A getting started list.** Six lines under the queue tick themselves as you go, with notifications and a design kit one click away.
- Preferences, sounds and phone moved to Settings. They were never needed to start.
