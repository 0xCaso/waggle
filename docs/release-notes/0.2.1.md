# Waggle 0.2.1

*Product teams were built around handoffs. What comes next is built around decisions.*

**Now called Waggle everywhere**

- If your app is still named Knock.app, Waggle renames it to Waggle.app the first time it opens, then opens again. Finder, Spotlight, Launchpad and the Dock now say Waggle.
- After the rename, Waggle points your Claude Code and Codex plugins, the Claude app entry and your `waggle` and `knock` command links at the new place. Nothing to do by hand. If a step cannot run, Waggle keeps working and writes why in its migration log.

**Better**

- The waggle logo now sits at the top left of the queue, in line with the lists below.
