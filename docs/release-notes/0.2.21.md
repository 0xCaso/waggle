# Waggle 0.2.21

- **Comment right on the request.** You can now pin comments on a prototype, screenshots or a write-up straight from the request page, the same way as in full size. Press C or pick Comment, click or drag, write your note. Your comments show as pins and in the list under the artifact, and go with your reply.
- **Tidier option rows.** In a tight row the Recommended pill shrinks to ✦ instead of cutting its word.
