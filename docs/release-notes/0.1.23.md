# Knock 0.1.23

*Your agents knock before they ship. Defuse the slop before it lands.*

**See your agents**

- **Sessions panel.** Click "Connected · N sessions" at the bottom of the list to see every running session: its project and branch, what it is doing now, and how many of its asks need you. Click one to see its asks today, show only its asks in the list, or copy its folder path.

**A calmer review window**

- **Tools on the canvas.** Interact and Comment moved into a small toolbar at the bottom of the canvas (keys I and C). Hover a tool to see what it does. In Comment mode the pointer shows "Drag to flag" until your first comment.
- **Question and Comments tabs.** The right column has its own switch, so you can read the question while you pin comments. Placing a pin opens Comments.

**Fixed**

- **Previews never spin forever.** If a prototype takes too long to draw, the card says "Preview is taking long. Open full size" after 10 seconds.
- **Click to type.** Clicking anywhere in the reply box puts the cursor in it, and the pointer shows you can type.
- **The answer card fits the review column** on one row, with a short "Accept" button when space is tight.

**After updating:** nothing to do.
