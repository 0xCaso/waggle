# Knock 0.1.29

*Your agents knock before they ship. Defuse the slop before it lands.*

**See what each session is doing**

- **Sessions panel with context.** Each Claude Code session now shows its own title and what it is doing right now: the step of its plan, or its last action in plain words. Open a session to see its goal, what it just said, its recent actions and the files it changed. Knock reads this from the session's transcript and git on your Mac. Nothing leaves your machine. If a session needs you, that still comes first.

**Screenshots you can see whole**

- **The whole screen, not a 3x crop.** Phone screenshots now fit the stage by default. Use Fit, 100% and the zoom buttons (⌘0, ⌘1, ⌘+, ⌘−) to look closer, and Option-drag to pan.
- **Move through a set.** A slim strip under the stage shows "2 / 4" and the screen's name. Use ← and → to move between screens. "Set" shows every screen side by side; click one to open it.

**After you answer**

- **A toast in the top right.** After you send, a small card shows "Sent · waiting for pickup" with your answer, then "Delivered" when the agent has it. "Go back" and "Change answer" are right there. It is gone within 10 seconds.
- **When an agent does not pick up your reply.** After 2 minutes, Knock offers "Copy prompt" (paste "Check Knock for my reply" in its terminal) and "Show the terminal".

**Also**

- **Badge on the Dock icon** with the number of asks that need you.

**Fixed**

- **A new round starts with an empty reply box.** Your previous answer and its comments no longer come back when the agent sends a new version.

**Try it early (off by default):** Knock can nudge a Claude Code session that has not picked up your reply, straight into the session. To turn it on, run `defaults write dev.limone.knock labs.nudge -bool true` and restart Knock. A "Nudge" button then shows next to "Copy prompt", for Claude Code sessions started after the update.

**After updating:** nothing to do.
