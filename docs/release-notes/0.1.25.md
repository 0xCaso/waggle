# Knock 0.1.25

*Your agents knock before they ship. Defuse the slop before it lands.*

**A list that stays honest**

- **Answered group.** "Waiting on agent" now only holds asks that are really waiting: a reply not picked up yet, a failed delivery, or an agent still working on your reply. Ten minutes after the agent picks up your reply, or when its session closes, the ask moves to a collapsed "Answered" group at the bottom of the list, grouped by day. Click "Show" to see them.
- **Agents running.** The "All caught up" screen now shows how many agents are running, instead of a count that only ever grew.

**Smoother reviews**

- **No flash when you switch tools.** The prototype stays exactly where you left it (the screen you clicked into, what you typed) when you switch between Interact and Comment. Comments are drawn on top of the live page.
- **No empty captures.** A capture only appears once it has a comment.
- **Delete and edit.** Each capture has a trash icon, and each comment can be edited or deleted.

**After updating:** nothing to do.
