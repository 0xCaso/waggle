# Knock 0.1.24

*Your agents knock before they ship. Defuse the slop before it lands.*

**Agent orbs**

- **One orb per agent.** Each ask in the list, each session in the sessions panel and in Settings, and the footer show a small living orb. Its colour says whose turn it is; its motion says what the agent is doing: it swells while it waits for you, is almost still until your reply is picked up, turns while the agent works, pulses while it writes the detail you asked for, and goes still and grey when the session closes.
- **What the agent is doing.** When the ask you have open is waiting on the agent, a card at the top says so: working on your reply, not picked up yet, writing the detail you asked for, or closed before it read your reply.
- The orbs pause when Knock is in the background or Reduce Motion is on.

**Fixed**

- **Sessions stay live.** Your agent sessions now reconnect to Knock on their own after an update or a restart, instead of showing as closed until their next ask. This starts working after this update is installed.
- **Tidier Agents settings.** Even spacing between Claude Code and Codex.
