# Knock 0.1.55

*Your agents knock before they ship. Defuse the slop before it lands.*

**New**

- Pinch to zoom works on screenshots, diagrams and prototypes. Pinch in to look closer, and pinch out to see the whole thing again.
- A plan round can show any kind of artifact. A brainstorm can now put a diagram, a prototype, a screenshot or a write-up next to its question.

**Fixed**

- The stage track in a plan ask fits narrow windows. It picks the form that suits the width you have.
- Long explanations wrap in narrow windows. A paragraph no longer runs past the edge, also after you resize the window and when you look at an older version.
