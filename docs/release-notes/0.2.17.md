# Waggle 0.2.17

- **Plan pages side by side.** A plan's write-up or prototype fills the left column at full height and scrolls on its own; the question and your options sit on the right. Narrow windows stack, with the write-up open.
- **Options always in the bottom bar.** One line per option with Good and Watch counts; hover to see the lists. Your pick shows in the page with its description, lists and why.
- **Open any artifact bigger.** Every artifact has an Open menu: Full size, New window, or the browser.
- **A clearer need box.** The question shows first in semibold, the details as paragraphs, with bold and lists from the agent.
- **Plain titles.** The line above a decision shows the session title, not the folder name, and the section label shows once.
