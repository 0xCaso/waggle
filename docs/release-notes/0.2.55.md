# Waggle 0.2.55

- **Send a screenshot to a session.** Take a screenshot, then send it to a live agent session with a short note. Turn it on in Settings › Screenshots. It is off until you turn it on.
- **Two ways to start.** "After every screenshot" shows a small "Send to an agent" badge above the screenshot thumbnail for a few seconds. "Only with a shortcut" waits for Control Option S, or "Send a screenshot" in the menu bar, and shows your 3 latest screenshots.
- **Only your screenshots.** Waggle looks only at screenshots you take, never at your screen. It sends the file path and your note to the session. Nothing leaves your Mac.
