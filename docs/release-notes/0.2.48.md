# Waggle 0.2.48

- **No more freeze after you answer (issue 9).** A write-up box could be built again and again, and each time the whole page was laid out again. Now a rebuilt box keeps its state, so the page settles.
- **One click picks an option.** After a short rest on an option, its tooltip could take your click, so you had to click again. Now the option is picked as you press.
- **Screenshots follow the decision in full size.** When you answer one decision and the next one comes up, the screenshot set flips to that decision's image, as it already did in the box.
