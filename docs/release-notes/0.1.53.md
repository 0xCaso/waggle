# Knock 0.1.53

*Your agents knock before they ship. Defuse the slop before it lands.*

**New**

- Answer asks from your phone. Connect Telegram or Slack in Settings > Phone, step by step. New asks arrive with one button per option, you can also reply in your own words, and the answer goes back to the same agent. The Mac shows "Answered from Telegram" or "Answered from Slack".
- Off until you connect it. Your tokens stay in the Keychain on this Mac, and Knock talks only to Telegram or Slack, and only after you connect them. There is no Knock server in between, so your Mac needs to be awake for a phone answer to land.

**Fixed**

- Zooming a prototype out now fills the stage, instead of shrinking the page into the top-left corner.
- When an agent updates an ask but keeps the old pictures, the stage now says so: "Still showing v1 · not updated with the 15:36 change".
- After "Ask for more detail", your sent reply now reads "Asked for more detail · ..." instead of an empty "You:".
- Typing a space in the reply box works again when the pointer rests on a prototype.
