# Knock 0.1.59

*Your agents knock before they ship. Defuse the slop before it lands.*

**New**

- Knock in the Claude app and Cowork. In Settings > Agents, Claude is now one row with two places: Claude Code, and the Claude app and Cowork. Press Connect to add Knock to the Claude app: Knock shows you the exact file it changes, keeps a backup, and Remove takes it out again. Optional, nothing changes until you press it.
- Agents in the Claude app can send documents and prototypes straight into an ask, without a project folder, and name their asks by topic so they group together in your queue.
- Starter prompts. Settings > Agents has ready prompts for research, product marketing and design. Pick a role, copy one, paste it into your agent.
- Design kit. Put a `.knock/design-kit.md` in a project to point your agents at its design system, Pen file and tone of copy. Optional: without it nothing changes.

**Faster**

- Clicking quickly through asks settles in about a second and a half.
