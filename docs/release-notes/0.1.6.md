# Knock 0.1.6

*Your agents knock before they ship. Defuse the slop before it lands.*

**Agents that know how to knock**

- **Your answers don't get missed.** Agents now keep waiting for your reply instead of moving on and forgetting the ask.
- **Asks that make sense on their own.** Agents spell out every option in the ask itself, keep "why" short, and put their reasoning, plans and charts in an attachment you can open full size.
- **"Ask me in Knock" just works.** Say "ask me in Knock", "send me a review" or "check with me", and the agent asks you in Knock instead of in the terminal.
- **A built-in guide for agents.** Agents can look up how to write a good ask, which attachment to use (with ready templates), how to ask for a UI review, and what to do after your reply.
- **Clearer errors.** If Knock isn't running, your agent tells you in plain words to open it.

**After updating:** restart any Claude Code or Codex session that was open before, so it gets the new guidance.
