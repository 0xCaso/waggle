# Knock 0.1.22

*Your agents knock before they ship. Defuse the slop before it lands.*

**On to the next ask**

- **Next ask after you answer.** When you send a reply, Knock opens the next ask that needs you and shows "Answer sent" with how many are left. Go back takes you to the one you just answered.
- **All caught up.** When nothing needs you, Knock says so, with how many asks you answered today, your median time to decide and how many are waiting on agents.

**A cleaner answer box**

- **One card.** The options, your note and the buttons now sit together in one box, in the ask and in the review window.
- **Numbered options.** Options are numbered 1, 2, 3. Press the number to pick one (when you're not typing), ⌘↵ to send, ⌘D to accept the recommendation.
- **No double labels.** An option written as "A · Compact" now reads just "Compact", in the options and in the Recommended block. Agents are also told to stop adding their own letters.

**Fixed**

- **The session count is right.** The sidebar counted every session Knock had ever seen. It now counts only the ones connected right now. Settings, Agents lists open sessions, then the five most recently closed.
