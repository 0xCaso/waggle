# Knock 0.1.36

*Your agents knock before they ship. Defuse the slop before it lands.*

**Copy anything**

- **Select and copy what agents write.** Every message, the question, the recommendation and the options can be selected and copied.
- **One-click Copy.** Hover a message in the conversation to copy it. Long commands get their own Copy button.

**Updates you can't miss**

- **A new update shows in the ask.** When the conversation is folded, the agent's latest update appears above "Need from you", with any command in a block you can copy. "See conversation" opens it at that message.

**Fold both sides at once**

- **The expand button folds both sidebars** to their slim rails instead of going full screen. Click it again, or press Esc, to bring back what you had. ⌘\ does the same.

**After updating:** nothing to do.
