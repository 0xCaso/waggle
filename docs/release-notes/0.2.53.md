# Waggle 0.2.53

- **Comments after you decide.** When your team comments on a shared case after you decided it, the case shows "N comments since you decided". Choose Change answer to open the case again (the shared page shows it as open until you answer), or Send to the agent to give the comments to your agent. If the agent's session is closed, copy the prompt and paste it in a new session.
- **Late comments wait for you.** A comment that comes after your decision no longer wakes the agent by itself. You decide when the agent sees it.
