# Knock 0.1.40

*Your agents knock before they ship. Defuse the slop before it lands.*

**Tell agents what to bring you**

- Setup has two new steps. **What can you use Knock for?** shows ideas beyond code: marketing, design, operations, finance and more. **What should they bring you?** lets you pick topics, how often agents ask, and how much detail they add, or just choose Anything.
- **Settings › Asks** keeps the same choices, with **Brief** or **Rich** detail. Agents get your preferences when a session starts.

**More context on every ask**

- Each ask now shows what its session is working on and the earlier asks, answers and updates from that session, so you can answer without the chat.

**Fixes**

- Comment pins stay on the prototype tab where you made them. A small chip tells you when other tabs have comments.
- An ask you answered long ago no longer shows "Working".
- The footer session count recovers at once after the daemon restarts or Knock updates.

**After updating:** open Settings › Asks to set your preferences, then start a new agent session.
