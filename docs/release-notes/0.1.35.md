**Pinch to zoom on screenshots**

- **Zoom with the trackpad.** Pinch on a screenshot to zoom in and out around your fingers. Two-finger scroll moves around. A two-finger double-tap switches between Fit and 100%.
- **One zoom, many ways.** Pinch, Fit, − and +, and ⌘+ / ⌘− / ⌘0 all share the same zoom, and comment pins stay in place at every level.
