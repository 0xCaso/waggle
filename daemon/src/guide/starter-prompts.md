# Starter prompts for research, product marketing and design

Optional. These are short recipes a person can paste, or an agent can offer when the person
asks "what can I do with Waggle?". They are vague on purpose: fill the gaps by asking in Waggle,
not in the terminal. Nobody has to pick a role. Never ask the person for one.

Every recipe uses the same loop: do a first pass, send it with waggle_ask, read the reply
(waggle_check), apply it with waggle_update. Where a recipe says Pen, design in Pen, then send
the frames (see the artifacts topic, "Designed it in Pen?"). If the project has a design kit
(`.knock/design-kit.md`), its Pen file and brand values come first.

## Research

- **Waggle a research question.** "Waggle this question with me: <question>. Ask me which
  angle matters, collect what you find, and send each round as a markdown review I can comment on."
- **Interview guide, then a summary.** "Draft an interview guide for <topic> and send it for
  review. After I run the calls, I will paste notes: send back the themes with quotes."

## Product marketing

- **Variants of a deck in Pen.** "Take <deck or slide> and draw three variants in Pen. Send them
  as a screenshot gallery and let me pick one or mix parts."
- **One message, three tones.** "Write <message> in three tones. Send them as options in
  Waggle with your pick and why."

## Design

- **From idea to prototype in Pen.** "Start from this idea: <idea>. Ask me two or three
  questions, draw the key screens in Pen, send them for review, then build a clickable prototype."
- **Review a screen.** "Look at <screen or frame> and send me what feels off as a review,
  with one fix per point."

## Rules for the agent

- Offer these only when asked, or when the person seems unsure how to start. Never block a
  first ask on them.
- Keep the person's words. Do not turn a recipe into a form to fill in.
- Plain, friendly copy. No em-dashes.

## WRONG / right

WRONG: "Pick your role: researcher, marketer or designer." Roles gate nothing.

Right: "Here are a few ways to start. Want one of them, or something else?"
